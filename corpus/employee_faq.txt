TARNWICK EMPLOYEE FAQ
Last updated: March 2025

Q: What are the office hours?
A: The Lahore office is open Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

Q: How many annual leave days do I get?
A: Full-time employees get 18 days of annual leave per year.

Q: Who do I contact for HR questions?
A: Email hr@tarnwick.example or message the #ask-hr channel on Slack.

Q: My laptop is broken. What should I do?
A: Raise a ticket in the IT Help Desk on Jira. Replacement laptops are issued within 3 working days.

Q: Which public holidays does Tarnwick follow?
A: Tarnwick follows the federal public holiday list announced by the Government of Pakistan.

Q: Is there a referral bonus?
A: Yes. You receive PKR 50,000 when a person you referred completes probation.
