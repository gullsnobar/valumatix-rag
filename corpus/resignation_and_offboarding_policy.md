# Tarnwick Resignation and Offboarding Policy

Version 1.1 - Effective 1 July 2026 - Owner: HR Department

## 1. Notice Period
Confirmed employees must give 30 days of written notice. Employees on probation must give 7 days of notice.

## 2. Leave During Notice
Annual leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period unless the line manager agrees in writing. Unused annual leave is not paid out in cash.

## 3. Handover
Before the last day, the employee completes a handover document for their projects and clients, reviewed by their line manager.

## 4. Company Property
The laptop, access card and any other company equipment must be returned on the last working day.

## 5. Final Settlement
The final salary and any approved expense claims are paid within 30 days after the last working day. An experience letter is issued within 7 working days.

_Fictional document created for a RAG portfolio project. Tarnwick Consulting is an invented company._
