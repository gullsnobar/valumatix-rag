# Tarnwick Security and IT Policy

Version 3.0 - Effective 1 February 2026 - Owner: IT and Security

## 1. Passwords and Access
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on every company account.
- Store all work passwords in the company Bitwarden vault. Never share passwords over Slack or email.

## 2. Devices
- All company laptops use full-disk encryption (BitLocker).
- Security patches must be installed within 7 days of release.
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.
- Client data must never be stored on personal devices.

## 3. Incident Reporting
Report any suspected security incident, lost device or phishing email to security@tarnwick.example within 1 hour of noticing it.

## 4. Client Data Retention
Client data is deleted 90 days after a contract ends, unless the contract says otherwise.

## 5. AI Tools
Only approved AI tools may be used with client data. Never paste client credentials or personal data into public AI chatbots.
