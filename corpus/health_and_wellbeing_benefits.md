# Tarnwick Health and Wellbeing Benefits

Version 1.0 - Effective 1 January 2026 - Owner: HR Department

## 1. Medical Insurance
Group medical insurance covers the employee, their spouse and up to 2 children. It starts on the first day of employment.

| Cover | Limit per year |
| --- | --- |
| Hospitalisation | PKR 500,000 per family |
| Outpatient (OPD) | PKR 60,000 per family |
| Maternity | PKR 150,000 |

## 2. Gym Allowance
Employees can claim up to PKR 4,000 per month for a gym or sports membership, with a receipt.

## 3. Counselling
The confidential Employee Assistance Programme offers up to 6 free counselling sessions per year.

## 4. How to Claim
Submit outpatient claims through the insurer's app within 60 days of treatment. For hospitalisation, contact HR before admission unless it is an emergency.
