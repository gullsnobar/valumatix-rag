# Tarnwick Travel Policy

Version 1.1 - Effective 1 April 2026 - Owner: Operations

## 1. Booking
All business travel is booked through the TravelDesk team at travel@tarnwick.example. Flights must be requested at least 14 days before departure. Requests made later need written approval from your line manager.

## 2. Approval
Trips costing up to PKR 100,000 in total are approved by the line manager. Trips above PKR 100,000, and all international trips, need approval from the Operations Director.

## 3. Daily Allowances
Meal and hotel limits are set in the Expense and Reimbursement Policy. Employees on international trips receive a daily allowance of USD 60 instead of meal claims.

## 4. Insurance and Safety
Travel insurance is mandatory for every international trip and is arranged by TravelDesk. Share your itinerary with TravelDesk before you leave and check in by email when you arrive.

## 5. Changes and Cancellations
Ask TravelDesk to change or cancel a booking at least 48 hours before departure where possible. Penalties caused by personal changes are paid by the employee.
