# Tarnwick IT Help Desk Guide

Version 1.0 - Effective 1 March 2026 - Owner: IT

## 1. How to Get Help
Raise every IT request as a ticket in the IT Help Desk project on Jira. For urgent issues outside office hours, call the on-call IT line listed on the intranet.

## 2. Priority Levels and Response Times
| Priority | Example | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 - Critical | Company-wide outage, security incident | 30 minutes |
| P2 - High | You cannot work at all | 2 hours |
| P3 - Normal | A tool or feature is not working | 1 business day |
| P4 - Low | Questions and small requests | 3 business days |

## 3. Laptops
A broken laptop is replaced within 3 working days. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## 4. Passwords
Use the self-service reset portal for forgotten passwords. IT will never ask for your password by email or chat.

## 5. Software Requests
New software must be approved by IT before installation. Free tools are reviewed within 5 business days.
