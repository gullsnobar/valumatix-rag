# Tarnwick Learning and Development Policy

Version 1.2 - Effective 1 January 2026 - Owner: HR Department

## 1. Annual Budget
Every confirmed full-time employee has a learning budget of PKR 150,000 per calendar year. Unused budget does not roll over.

## 2. Learning Days
Employees receive 5 paid learning days per year for courses, conferences or exam preparation. Learning days are separate from annual leave.

## 3. Certifications
Certification exam fees are reimbursed after you pass the exam. Approved certifications include Salesforce, HubSpot, AWS and project management credentials. A failed exam is reimbursed once if the retake is passed within 6 months.

## 4. Repayment
If the company spends more than PKR 75,000 on your training in a year and you resign within 12 months, you repay 50% of that amount.

## 5. How to Apply
Submit a learning request in PeopleDesk at least 3 weeks before the course starts. Your line manager approves requests within the budget.
