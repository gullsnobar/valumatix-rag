# Tarnwick Performance Review Policy

Version 2.0 - Effective 1 January 2026 - Owner: HR Department

## 1. Review Cycle
Tarnwick runs two formal performance reviews per year: a mid-year review in June and an annual review in December.

## 2. Rating Scale
| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Exceptional - far above expectations |
| 4 | Strong - above expectations |
| 3 | Solid - meets expectations |
| 2 | Developing - partly meets expectations |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory - does not meet expectations |

## 3. Salary Increments
Salary increments are decided at the December review and take effect on 1 January. Employees rated 1 or 2 are not eligible for an increment that year.

## 4. Performance Improvement Plan
An employee rated 1 is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with written goals and fortnightly check-ins with their manager.

## 5. Promotions
Promotions are considered only at the December review. An employee must have been in their current role for at least 12 months.
